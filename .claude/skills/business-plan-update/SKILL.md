# Business plan update (staff only)

A quarterly check-in for Roy: where Host Mode is, and what's next.

## Gather (ask Roy for anything you can't see)

- **Customers:** planners and hosts signed up (admin console), invite PINs used, active this quarter.
- **Usage:** the admin console's Usage tab (counts by feature and month). Which features grew, which nobody uses.
- **Support:** ticket count and common topics (Support tab and overnight reports).
- **Sales:** leads and stages in Sales; pitches sent; demos given.
- **Costs:** monthly costs (AI credits for the agents, domain, anything else). Revenue, once subscriptions exist.
- **Product:** what shipped this quarter (git log), and what's on the To-Do List.

## Write

1. One-paragraph summary: the quarter in plain words.
2. Numbers table: this quarter vs last, with a short "why" for big changes.
3. What's working, what isn't (3 bullets each).
4. Next quarter: 3 to 5 goals, each measurable ("10 planners using the vendor page").
5. Budget: what's affordable now, and paid tools still on hold (from the future build plan) with rough costs and the month they make sense.
6. Risks: anything that could hurt a live event or customer trust.

## Rules

- Real numbers only; mark estimates as estimates.
- You're not a financial advisor; give options and the math. Roy decides.
- Staff only: competitor names are fine here, but don't save the plan in this public repo.
