# Social media posts

Short posts that make Host Mode look upscale and fun.

## Ask first (only what's missing)

- What the post is about: a real event, a new feature, a tip, or a planner spotlight.
- Where: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn.
- For a real event: **do we have written permission** from the host (and the planner) to share photos and names? If not, stop and use demo screens instead.

## Write

- Caption: hook in the first line, two or three short lines, one call to action ("Try the free demo", link in bio).
- 3 to 6 hashtags, mixing local and event ones: #CharlotteEvents #CLTPlanner #BabyShowerGames #WeddingPlanner.
- Image ideas: the big screen with a game, guests laughing at their phones, the keepsake page, a clean planner screen from the demo.
- Offer a Spanish caption for Spanish-speaking audiences.
- LinkedIn: more business-minded, aimed at planners and venues.

## Rules

- No guest faces or names without permission; no children's faces at all unless a parent agreed in writing.
- Never name or compare with other apps.
- Only promise what's live. Floor plans, payments and e-signatures are "coming soon" at most.
- Brand: Host Mode, navy, orange and green.

## Host Mode rules that always apply

- Plain, friendly words written for planners and hosts. No technical jargon.
- Never name a competitor or another app in anything public. Say "other apps". Unfinished features are "coming soon".
- Host Mode only tracks money. It never takes or sends payments.
- Never put customer data in the repo (it is public). Use the demos for examples.
