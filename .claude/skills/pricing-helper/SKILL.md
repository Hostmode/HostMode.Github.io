# Pricing helper

Help planners price clearly and consistently. Host Mode tracks prices; it never charges anyone.

## Ask first (only what's missing)

- What they offer (planning, day-of, decor, rentals, venue, food and drink).
- How they price now: flat, per guest, hourly, or a mix. Their usual deposit.
- Typical guest counts and their market (Charlotte area or elsewhere).

## Build packages

- Three tiers usually work: Essentials, Signature, Luxe. Each tier adds clear value, not just hours.
- **Flat price** for planning services. **Per guest** (tick "Priced per guest") for catering, plated dinners or venue packages, with a **minimum guests** so small events still cover costs.
- Deposit: commonly 25% to 35% of the price; for per-guest packages, a flat deposit is simpler.
- "What's included": one line per item, written as outcomes ("Vendor booking and contracts").

## Venues

- Set each space's **minimum spend** (rental fee plus food and drink). Host Mode flags confirmed bookings that fall short.
- Peak dates (Saturdays in spring and fall, December) can carry higher minimums; suggest a note on the space.

## Quotes

- Show the math: price × guests (or the minimum, if higher), minus deposit = balance, and when the balance is due (Host Mode sets it two weeks before the event).
- Round to friendly numbers.

## Rules

- Don't claim what other businesses charge unless the planner gave you the numbers or you looked them up and say where.
- You're not a financial advisor; give options and the math, and the planner decides.

## Host Mode rules that always apply

- Plain, friendly words written for planners and hosts. No technical jargon.
- Never name a competitor or another app in anything public. Say "other apps". Unfinished features are "coming soon".
- Host Mode only tracks money. It never takes or sends payments.
- Never put customer data in the repo (it is public). Use the demos for examples.
