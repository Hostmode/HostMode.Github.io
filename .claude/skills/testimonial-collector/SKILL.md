# Testimonial collector

Good reviews, collected the honest way.

## After an event

1. Check the Feedback tab: who rated it 4 or 5, and who ticked that their words can be shared (`quote`).
2. For happy people who haven't left feedback: draft a short, warm ask (text or email) with the feedback link. One ask, no pressure, no reward for a good review.
3. For feedback below 4: don't ask for a review. Draft a caring follow-up for the planner instead.

## Turning reviews into quotes

- Use only reviews where the person allowed sharing.
- Keep their words. You may shorten with "…" but never change the meaning or add praise.
- Credit as they agreed: first name and event type ("Margaret, 50th birthday"), never a full name or photo without permission.
- Pick quotes that name something specific ("The trivia on the big screen had the whole room laughing").

## Where they go

- The planner's own reviews → their Showcase page (Showcase tab, reviews section).
- Reviews about Host Mode itself → Roy's Resources tab for sales, with the person's permission noted.

## Rules

- Never write a review for someone, invent a quote, or present a demo review as real.
- Never name other apps.
