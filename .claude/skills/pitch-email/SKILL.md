# Write a Host Mode pitch

For Icee and anyone selling Host Mode. Write one message the person can send as themselves.

## Ask first (only what's missing)

- Who it's to: name, business, and what they are (planner, venue, decorator or rentals, vendor, single-event host).
- Where it goes: email, text, Instagram or LinkedIn message.
- What you know about them: how many events, what they use now, how you found them, anything they said.
- Is this the first message or a follow-up? If a follow-up, what happened last time?

## The message

- **Short.** Email: 90 to 140 words plus a subject line. Text or DM: 2 to 4 sentences.
- **Open with them**, not with Host Mode: something specific about their business or a problem people like them have.
- **One problem, one answer.** Pick the hook that fits who they are:
  - Planners: they pay for several apps and nothing helps on the event day. Host Mode plans it and runs the party.
  - Venues: bookings, blocked days and rentals in one calendar that warns before anything is double-booked.
  - Decorators and rentals: inventory with pull lists that warn when you're short, and check-in that catches missing pieces.
  - Vendors: one page per event with their arrival time and the live schedule.
  - Single-event hosts: games, a big screen and check-in that make the party feel professional.
- **One proof point**, from what's live today: "bring everything over in one upload", "real help right inside the app",
  "every vendor gets one page with the live schedule", "guests play on their phones and the big screen".
- **One easy ask**: a 15-minute look, a demo link, or "can I send you a 2-minute video?" Never two asks.
- **Sign off** with the sender's name, then "Host Mode · Charlotte, NC".

## Rules (these are firm)

- Never name a competitor or another app. Say "other apps" or "the apps you use now."
- Only promise what's live. Floor plans, payments, email reminders and e-signatures are "coming soon" at most; usually leave them out.
- No prices or discounts until subscriptions launch. If asked, say pricing is coming and early users will hear first.
- No pressure, no fake urgency, no exaggeration. Warm, plain, confident. No jargon.
- Spanish: if they write or prefer Spanish, write the whole message in natural Spanish.

## Follow-ups

- Wait about 3 business days after a first message, then a week.
- Each follow-up adds something new: a short demo video, a tip for their busy season, or an answer to something they said.
- After three follow-ups with no reply, write a friendly last note that leaves the door open, then set them to "Not now" in Sales.

## After writing

Offer one alternative subject line, and remind the sender to log the message in Sales (staff area → Sales → the person → Log it).

## Example (email to a planner)

Subject: Your event day, finally in the same app

Hi Dana,

You mentioned you're juggling three apps plus a rentals spreadsheet for about 30 weddings a year. Host Mode puts clients, planning, inventory and the wedding day itself in one place, with games and a big screen guests love, and each vendor gets one page with their arrival time and the live schedule.

Your old spreadsheets come over in one upload. Could I show you in 15 minutes this week?

Icee
Host Mode · Charlotte, NC
