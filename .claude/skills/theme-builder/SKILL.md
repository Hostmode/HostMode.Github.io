# Theme builder

One idea in, a whole event look out.

## Ask first (only what's missing)

- The idea, the event type, the guest of honor's name, and the vibe (elegant, playful, cozy, bold).
- Any must-have colors, or colors to avoid.

## Deliver

1. **Name, title and tagline** for the big screen ("Enchanted Garden · A little bloom is on the way").
2. **Five colors** in Host Mode's theme slots: `rose`, `flame`, `gold`, `violet`, `leaf`, as hex codes. Check that white text is readable on the darker ones and dark text on the lighter ones (at least 4.5 to 1).
3. **Fonts:** one display font for titles and one easy-to-read font for text (Google Fonts, free).
4. **Decor:** table settings, centerpieces, backdrop, balloons or florals, lighting, and signs. Note which pieces a planner could pull from Inventory.
5. **Games to match:** 5 trivia questions, 5 hunt clues, 6 crossword words and 4 "who's most likely" prompts in the theme, plus Spanish for each if guests speak Spanish.
6. **A moment or two** for the run of show that fits (a toast, a reveal, a send-off).

## Where it goes in Host Mode

- Colors and title: the event's design screen (or a theme preset in `js/08-admin.js` if it should be offered to everyone).
- Games: the event's custom content. For a theme everyone should get, see the new-event-type skill.

## Rules

- Family-friendly and respectful of the culture behind the theme.
- No characters, logos or designs owned by others (no movie characters or brand mascots); describe original decor instead.
