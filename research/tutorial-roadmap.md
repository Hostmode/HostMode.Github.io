# Tutorial roadmap

All 28 proposed topics exist in the code (see research/feature-verification.md). None are cut. Changes:

- **T02** waits for a test invite code.
- **T10 and T24** are retitled to "Tracking Vendors and Payments": Host Mode records payments but never takes or sends money. Card payments and email are on hold.
- **T13** covers opening the big-screen link on a TV or laptop browser. There's no casting feature, so the video won't imply one.
- **T15** covers joining by link or QR only. Invitations and RSVPs live in the separate invite app.
